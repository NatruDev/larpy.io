Questions

Is Larpy free?

Yes. The timer, streaks, feed and stats are free.

Can I use it before accounts open?

Yes. The timer works in your browser today, with no sign-up. Sessions are saved in that browser, so save a share card of anything you want to keep.

Why is it called Larpy?

Larping means acting the part. Larpy is the opposite: the time on your posts is time you actually studied. The name is the only thing here that’s pretend.

Does it work on my phone?

It works in your phone’s browser now. The iPhone and Android apps come after accounts open, and the waitlist hears first.

What do you do with my email?

We email you when accounts open, plus a few launch updates. We never sell or share it. Reply to any of our emails and we’ll delete it.
